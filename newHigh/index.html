Each row is a trading day. The numbers count stocks that traded that day at their highest (New highs) or lowest (New lows) level of the last 5, 14, 21 (1 month), 63 (3 months), 126 (6 months) or 252 (1 year) trading days. Click a day or a number to see the stocks. Stocks at a 1-year extreme also appear in every shorter period. Upload the yearly files made by the script (select all of them at once), or a single price CSV with columns symbol, date, close (optional high and low). Data stays in your browser. Sample data is randomly generated, not real prices.
